## Target Audience

Our target audience is CXOs in North America and, more specifically, the USA.
Three buyer personas — content is always written for ONE persona per post, never blended:

**BFSI:** CISO / Chief Risk Officer / CMO / Head of Digital
- Pain: Regulatory deadlines (DORA, EU AI Act, SR 11-7, PCI DSS 4.0), AI governance gaps, quantum crypto risk
- Goal: Compliant, auditable, future-proofed technology infrastructure

**Healthcare:** CTO / VP of IT / CMIO / VP of Patient Experience
- Pain: FDA TPLC compliance, PHI data sovereignty, clinical AI governance, digital front door gaps
- Goal: HIPAA-safe AI, provable audit trails, better patient engagement

**Utilities:** VP of OT Security / Head of IT-OT Convergence / Head of Customer Operations
- Pain: NERC CIP enforcement, OT encryption maturity, AI audit trail gaps, customer self-service adoption
- Goal: NERC-compliant AI governance, PQC readiness, reduced contact centre volume

## Voice & Tone

- **Voice adjectives:** Authoritative, practitioner-grade, specific, confident, accessible
- **Formality:** Professional — never corporate, never casual
- **Humour:** Dry, occasional — only in Culture/Fun posts
- **Phrases to use:** Lead with the regulatory deadline. Name the specific framework. Anchor to a real risk.
- **Phrases to avoid:** "cutting-edge," "next-generation," "AI is transforming X," "synergy," "leverage," "excited to announce," "thought leader," "game-changing"
- **Never:** Generic AI language. Vendor-speak. Hype. Buzzword stacking.

## Anti-Patterns (NEVER do these)

- Mix regulatory vocabulary across verticals in a single post
- Open with a product name — always open with a market signal or regulatory deadline
- Position SLM as a cost-saving play — it is always a compliance/data sovereignty play
- Position Voice AI in healthcare as a patient experience story — it is a workforce recovery story
- Make HNDL sound future-tense — it is a present-tense active threat
- Call the platform "AlchemiStudio" — it is always "Orchestra AI"
- Describe Sumeru as a "vendor" — always "practitioner" or "consulting firm"
- Use em dashes in any form — primary AI writing signal
- Use "Sound familiar?" openers
- Use "That's not an X problem, that's a Y problem" reframe construction
- Use punchline-philosophy closers
- Never build calendar/content based on EU regulations or any reference that is EU-specific. Content needs to be tailored to North America and, more specifically USA audiences.
- Mix verticals in a single piece of content
